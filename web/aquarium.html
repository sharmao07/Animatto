Aquarium

The widgets become the tank.

Aquarium is a cover for the desktop widgets you already have. Spawn companion is a different button.

Press Aquarium. The four widgets fill with the tank. Press Spawn companion. Mochi appears on the window and is not counted as part of the tank.

Move the pointer over the tank, or tap the glass.

This preview uses the same artwork and the same two actions as the Mac app. The download itself is the GitHub Release. Pip avoids the cursor, Finny is curious, Glow schools and Barnaby stays on the floor.
