⚙️ Engine Architecture

Engineered natively for the Apple ecosystem.

Animatto avoids heavy Electron sandboxes and sluggish web wrappers. Written directly in Swift with Cocoa and AppKit, it runs efficiently as an unobtrusive desktop citizen.

🪟

Transparent NSPanel Surfaces

Companions inhabit borderless, non-activating floating `NSPanel` layers at screen-saver levels. They freely roam without stealing keyboard focus or interrupting your active workspace.

📐

Physical Window Collision

Using macOS Accessibility and CoreGraphics window queries, companions detect the top coordinates of active application windows, allowing them to stand, walk, and leap atop your code editors and browser tabs.

🧠

Phase 6 Personality Matrix

Every companion runs an autonomous behavior state machine governed by 10 archetypes. Dynamic internal needs (Energy, Happiness, Curiosity, and Boredom) guide their movement and naps.

💬

Phase 9 Social Interaction Network

When companions roam within proximity of each other, they initiate coordinated mutual conversations, share greetings, celebrate accomplishments, or tease one another with contextual dialogue.

✨

On-Device Apple Vision ML

The built-in Workshop utilizes Apple's neural Vision framework to automatically extract sprites and remove solid backgrounds locally on your Neural Engine with zero cloud dependencies.

⏱

Phase 10 Focus Sessions

A built-in Pomodoro productivity timer coordinates your companions' behavior. During focus periods, companions quietly curl up to sleep; when break starts, they celebrate with cheerful hops.
