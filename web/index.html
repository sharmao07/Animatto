Original Artwork

Meet the companions.

Every character is custom-animated with original pixel artwork, authentic physics, and its own personality profile.

Mochi

Surface Roamer

Wanders across window titlebars, naps when you focus, purrs, and walks along the menu bar.

Finny

Golden Sunfin

Lively open-water swimmer. Curious, follows cursor motion, and darts upward for food pellets.

Pip

Pygmy Puffer

Chubby and cautious. Avoids sudden glass taps and prefers hovering near tank plants.

Glow

Ghost Tetra

Luminescent schooling fish. Synchronizes swimming rhythm with companions in the tank.

Barnaby

Hermit Crab

Patient benthic explorer. Crawls along the gravel floor and inspects mossy river stones.

Open Core Architecture

Free is genuinely useful.

The entire core companion application is Apache-2.0 open source. All companions, physics, desktop aquarium, ambient worlds, timers, and offline privacy features are free forever.
