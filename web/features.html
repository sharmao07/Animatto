Features

What is in the app.

Companions
Roaming, walking, running, jumping, falling, gravity, surface collision, window traversal, menu bar walking, drag and fling, multiple companions.
Personality
Ten archetypes with needs (energy, happiness, curiosity, boredom), contextual dialogue, quiet mode and anti-spam behaviour. Preview archetypes
Memory and social
Local memory of interactions and companion-to-companion encounters. Stored on your Mac.
Aquarium
Four original species, schooling, feeding, glass taps, bubbles and plants, themes and resizable windows. Try it
Ambient Worlds
Rain, snow, fog, sunlight beams, wind dust, falling leaves, cherry blossom petals, fireflies, rising steam and ocean waves. Eight built-in presets (Rainy Day, Enchanted Forest, Winter Calm, Sunset, Cosmic Dream, Sakura Garden, Cozy Café, Ocean), custom presets, .animattopack export and import, Open-Meteo weather sync, Inverse Weather Mode and a four-rarity collectible minigame.
Creator Studio
Magic Eraser (Apple Vision, on-device), AI sprite generation, community browser and imports. Details
Productivity
Focus timers (5, 15, 30, 60 minutes) and Pomodoro (25/5, 50/10). Try it
Audio
Footsteps, purring and aquarium sounds with master mute.
Integrations
Discord Rich Presence, Spotify and Apple Music status, GitHub Releases update checks. Privacy Shield stops background monitors instantly.
Accessibility & Languages
Reduced Motion support and ambient atmosphere localization across 11 languages: English, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, and Brazilian Portuguese.
