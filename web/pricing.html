Planned activation

  1. Pay through a hosted checkout ($3.99 for Lifetime Pro or $5.99 for Founder Supporter).
  2. The server confirms payment and issues a cryptographically signed license key.
  3. Enter the key in Animatto.
  4. The app validates the signed entitlement offline.
