Ambient Worlds

Change the weather on your Mac.

In the app, particles render in a click-through overlay and companions react to the atmosphere.

Animatto — Ambient Worlds Studio
CoreGraphics · Low Power

Click-Through Desktop Atmosphere

Ambient Worlds renders dynamic particle systems across your display while remaining completely click-through. Every click and gesture passes cleanly to whatever application you are working in.

  • Nine Handcrafted PresetsRainy Day, Enchanted Forest, Winter Calm, Sunset, Cosmic Dream, Sakura Garden, Cozy Café, Ocean, and Fireflies.
  • Low Power Mode AwareAutomatically clamps maximum particle counts and throttles physics loops when running on battery or in macOS Low Power Mode.
  • Open-Meteo Weather SyncOptionally synchronizes atmospheric conditions with real-world weather at your location.

Companion Reactions & Localization

Companions aren't static—they actively notice atmospheric changes. During rain, companions shake off water droplets or seek shelter; during quiet snowfalls, they curl up and purr.

  • 11 Languages LocalizedFull native atmospheric translations across English, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, and Portuguese.
  • Pack Import/ExportDesign custom atmospheric themes and share them via portable .animattopack files.
