Animatto — Productivity & Pomodoro Focus
Work Intervals

Focused Deep Work Cycles

Animatto coordinates companion behavior with your work rhythm. When you start a Pomodoro session, your companion ceases energetic wandering and quietly naps near the edge of your screen.

  • Structured PomodoroConfigurable 25/5 and 50/10 focus-to-break intervals with gentle chime notifications.
  • Anti-DistractionSpeech bubbles and spontaneous animations are completely muted during focus sessions.
  • Break CelebrationsWhen your break timer rings, your companion jumps up and stretches, encouraging you to step away from the screen.

10 Personality Archetypes

Every companion is governed by a 10-dimension personality matrix that determines curiosity, boldness, social initiative, and dialogue tendencies.

  • ExplorerHigh curiosity; wanders across windows and inspects new applications.
  • CompanionBalanced, supportive presence that stays close to your primary workspace.
  • GuardianAttentive and quiet; keeps watchful vigil atop the menu bar.
  • Playful & MischievousEnergetic routines with boundary bouncing and occasional antics.
