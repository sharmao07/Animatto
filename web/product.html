Product

A living desktop for your Mac.

Animatto is a native Swift, SwiftUI and AppKit application that gives your Mac desktop companions and environments.

Animatto — Native Architecture
Swift 5.9 · macOS 14.6+
Platform
macOS 14.6 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel. Built natively with Swift, SwiftUI, and AppKit.
Where to get it
GitHub Releases, linked from the Download page on this website. There is no Mac App Store listing.
Companions
Borderless windows driven by a physics loop. They walk on application windows and can walk along the menu bar. Launch them from the library.
Aquarium
A separate switch. It covers the Clock, Weather, Calendar, and Battery widgets. Turning it off shows those widgets again. It does not turn a companion into a fish.
Ambient layer
Weather particles render in a click-through overlay, so clicks reach the apps beneath.
Control
The menu bar lists roaming companions and has the Aquarium switch.
