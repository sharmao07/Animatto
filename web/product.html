Product

A living desktop for your Mac.

Animatto is a native Swift, SwiftUI and AppKit application that gives your Mac desktop companions and environments.

Platform
macOS 14.6 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel. Mac only.
How it works
Companions are borderless transparent windows driven by a physics loop. They collide with surfaces, walk on application windows and can walk upside down along the menu bar.
Ambient layer
Weather particles render in a separate click-through overlay, so your clicks reach the apps beneath.
Control
A menu bar dashboard pauses, mutes and switches companions without opening the main window.
What makes it different
An open-source core, original hand-crafted sprites, personalities with needs and local memory, and an on-device Magic Eraser.
