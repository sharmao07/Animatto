🐾 How to Install Animatto (v1.2.0 Public Beta)

1. Installation:
   👉 Drag the Animatto app icon into the Applications folder shortcut in this window.

2. First Launch (macOS Gatekeeper authorization):
   Because Animatto is distributed independently outside the Mac App Store:
   👉 In Finder, navigate to your Applications folder.
   👉 Right-click (or Control-click) Animatto.app and select 'Open'.
   👉 When prompted by macOS, click 'Open' to grant authorization.
   👉 (Alternatively, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click 'Open Anyway' under Security).
   You only need to do this once on first launch.

3. Using Animatto:
   👉 Launch Animatto from your Applications folder or Spotlight.
   👉 Menu Bar: Click the paw icon in your menu bar to spawn/dismiss companions, toggle Aquarium Mode, or adjust ambient soundscapes.
   👉 Aquarium Mode: Click 'Turn On Aquarium Mode' to transform your desktop companion widgets directly into interactive aquariums with swimming fish, bubbles, and feeding!
   👉 Library & Workshop: Click 'Add from PC' to import custom GIF/PNG/Shimeji characters, or use Creator Studio to generate and edit sprites.

4. Uninstallation:
   👉 Quit Animatto (from the Menu Bar icon > Quit, or Cmd+Q in the main window).
   👉 In Finder, drag Animatto.app from Applications to the Trash.
   👉 (Optional) To clear local companion data and preferences, delete:
      ~/Library/Application Support/Animatto
      ~/Library/Preferences/com.dev.Animatto.plist

5. Privacy & Community Feedback:
   👉 Animatto is 100% offline-first with zero tracking, telemetry, or analytics.
   👉 Report bugs or submit feature suggestions privately and safely at:
      https://github.com/sharmao07/Animatto/issues
