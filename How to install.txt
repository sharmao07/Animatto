🐾 How to Install Animatto

1. Drag the Animatto app icon into the Applications folder shortcut.
2. First Launch (macOS Gatekeeper authorization):
   Because Animatto is distributed independently outside the Mac App Store:
   👉 In Finder, open your Applications folder.
   👉 Right-click (or Control-click) Animatto.app and select 'Open'.
   👉 When prompted, click 'Open' to grant authorization.
   (You only need to do this once on first launch.)

3. Adding Companion Characters:
   👉 Launch Animatto from your Applications folder.
   👉 In the Library window, click 'Add from PC' to import any GIF, animated PNG, or Shimeji character pack from your computer.
   👉 You can also use Workshop Studio to create, edit, or remove backgrounds from your own sprites!
